Questions answered

The details, without the guesswork

Understand responsibilities and constraints before you decide.

Do I need VPS or cPanel hosting?

Choose cPanel-style hosting for an easier website setup. Consider VPS when you need OS access, custom runtime services or additional control.

Can I choose an operating system?

Supported operating systems depend on the actual available server inventory and management model.

Who maintains my server?

That depends on whether the service is self-managed or contracted as managed. Confirm patching, security and backup responsibilities.
